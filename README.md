# Chris Nesbitt-Smith

The most useful thing on a senior architect's GitHub profile is not a list of skills. It is a list of decisions that turned out to be expensive, made visible, and survived contact with production.

Here are some of mine.

## What is on the ledger

**£790bn a day** of UK payments now settle on the [Bank of England's renewed RTGS service](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/payment-and-settlement/rtgs-renewal-programme). I framed the cloud architecture and authored the specification behind it; the National Audit Office cited the programme as a cross-government exemplar.

**112,000 prosecutor hours a year** returned to frontline criminal justice by the [Crown Prosecution Service Casework App](https://www.cps.gov.uk/), which I architected and built. The team won DevOps Project of the Year 2024 with [esynergy](https://esynergy.co.uk/). The work was carried out under legal professional privilege; the outcome is on the public record.

**The National Digital Exchange (NDX)**, announced from No 10 and cited in the [Blueprint for Modern Digital Government](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/a-blueprint-for-modern-digital-government), targets a £1.26bn share of the £45bn-per-year opportunity identified by the [State of Digital Government Review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/state-of-digital-government-review). I designed it end to end: architecture, evidence base, delivery path, first artefact. The companion sandbox, NDX:Try, removes the twelve-month lead time between idea and first evidence; security architecture was developed with NCSC.

**A £350M AIRR procurement routes analysis** authored end to end. I sit on the panel as technical advisor.

**Multi-million pounds saved at the Home Office** by stopping the Common Data Platform programme; SRO approval secured, wind-down run, lessons distributed cross-government.

That is what the title "senior gov-tech architect" is supposed to mean. Most of the time it does not.

## Range, briefly

Ministers and Terraform. International advisory and admission-controller code. Secretary of State briefings on Monday, OPA Rego on Tuesday. The architect's job is to ride between the penthouse and the engine room, and to leave both floors better than they were found.

Penthouse: GDS Technical Assessor since the first iteration of the Service Standard, over 50 service assessments delivered, contributor to the Technology Code of Practice and Service Manual, NHS Compass working group, advisory work for Norwegian, Taiwanese and Scottish governments, European Commission correspondence, [Global Government Forum](https://www.globalgovernmentforum.com/) keynote.

Engine room: co-author of the Linux Foundation [LFS482 Zero Trust Security with SPIFFE and SPIRE](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/training/zero-trust-security-with-spiffe-and-spire-lfs482/) (with Control Plane), CKS, CKA, [Learnk8s](https://learnk8s.io/) instructor since 2018 to Apple, BP, Cisco, Dell, F5, JP Morgan, VMware and Vodafone. Built the cosign admission controller at Appvia that became a CNCF TAG Security reference pattern. Found and reported HIGH-severity OAuth CSRF, session-hijacking and JWT-replay flaws in production government systems through adversarial security review.

The gap between those two floors is where most architecture goes wrong. It is also where the work is.

## Public artefacts worth clicking

**[kubectl-passman](https://github.com/chrisns/kubectl-passman)** (131 stars). A kubectl plugin that bridges password managers (1Password, Keychain, GNOME Keyring, gopass) and `kubectl`. Real users, real install base, no theatre.

**[bsl-experiment](https://github.com/chrisns/bsl-experiment)**. Browser-side British Sign Language recognition. 18,871 signs, ONNX inference, four days; nobody wrote any code. Built end to end by Claude Code while I directed. Useful as both an accessibility prototype and an AI-native development reference.

**[apple-notes-to-obsidian](https://github.com/chrisns/apple-notes-to-obsidian)**. Reverse-engineered Apple's private Coherence framework via Swift shims into PaperKit's Paper bundle format. Preserves handwriting as editable vector strokes through the Ink plugin. Single-call data portability where Apple offers none.

**[docker-node-sonos-http-api](https://github.com/chrisns/docker-node-sonos-http-api)** (73 stars). Packaging that just works. There is a category of valuable software whose only job is to keep working; this is one of them.

**[childrens-clock](https://github.com/chrisns/childrens-clock)** (48 stars). ESP32 plus ESPHome plus NTP. A clock my children can read. The most-starred thing on this profile is a Sonos packaging container, the second is the kubectl secrets bridge, the third is a clock for a four-year-old. That order says something I find honest.

**[k8s-opa-boilerplate](https://github.com/chrisns/k8s-opa-boilerplate)** (18 stars). Open Policy Agent and kustomize boilerplate. The original policy-as-code seed before that phrase became a vendor category.

**[govreposcrape](https://github.com/chrisns/govreposcrape)** (17 stars). Semantic search over roughly 24,000 UK government repositories on Cloud Run plus Vertex AI Search. The companion [open-source dashboard](https://co-cddo.github.io/open-source-reporting/) has self-updated nightly since 2018.

**[enigma-machine](https://github.com/chrisns/enigma-machine)**. A laser-cut working Enigma replica. CAD, lithography, soldering. Architecture is a discipline that benefits from also knowing how things are physically made.

**[devpsyops](https://devpsyops.com/)**. A parody of `*-ops` vocabulary inflation. Has a unicorn. Becomes serious by the end. Most "best practices" are practices that optimise for someone else's constraint.
